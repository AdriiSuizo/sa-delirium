The Steam discovery design follows the documented libraryfolders.vdf and
appmanifest approach reviewed in rehan-remade/universal-modder at
9811709d950979f1b5012dddf10b7ec122d7e95e (um/scan.py).
The C# implementation and parser in this installer are newly written.

Long path handling follows Microsoft's Win32 extended-path documentation:
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/maximum-file-path-limitation

No third-party binaries, game executables, game assets, decompiled code,
ISO content, keys or personal saves are embedded in the installer.
Imported local content retains its original credits/license documents.
See the repository's CREDITS.md and licenses for the project's existing mod.
